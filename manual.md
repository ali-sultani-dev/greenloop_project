## 1. Introduction — What is GreenLoop?

GreenLoop is your company's official sustainability platform. It is a website you can open in any web browser — such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Microsoft Edge — on a computer, tablet, or phone. No app download is required.

Its purpose is to help every employee participate in your organisation's environmental goals in a fun, measurable, and rewarding way.

Think of GreenLoop as a **"fitness tracker for the planet."** Just as a fitness app tracks your steps and rewards your progress, GreenLoop tracks your eco-friendly actions at work, awards you points and badges, lets you compete on a company leaderboard, and unlocks real rewards as you level up.

### What can I do on GreenLoop?

| Feature | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Log green actions** | Record eco-friendly things you do at work — cycling to the office, reducing waste, saving energy, and more. |
| **Join challenges** | Compete in time-limited goals with colleagues individually or as a team. |
| **Earn badges** | Unlock digital achievement badges as you hit sustainability milestones. |
| **Claim rewards** | Exchange your level progress for real physical, digital, or experience prizes. |
| **Track your impact** | See charts showing your personal environmental impact over time. |
| **Work in teams** | Join or create a team and collaborate on team challenges. |
| **Read & learn** | Browse educational sustainability articles and company announcements. |

### Key Terms — Explained Simply

| Term | Plain-language explanation |
|---|---|
| **Points** | A numerical score you earn each time you log a sustainability action. More points = higher level. |
| **Green Score** | A separate measure of your total environmental impact, based on CO₂ equivalent saved. It goes up as you log high-impact actions. |
| **Level** | Your overall rank on the platform. You start at Level 1 and progress automatically as you earn more points. |
| **Badge** | A digital award unlocked automatically when you reach a specific milestone (for example, completing 10 actions). |
| **Reward** | A real-world prize (gift card, eco product, day off, etc.) you can claim once you reach a new level. |
| **Challenge** | A specific, time-limited goal you can join — for example, "Walk to work 5 times in 2 weeks." |
| **Action** | Any sustainability activity in the library that you can log to earn points. |
| **Admin** | A special type of user (usually a manager or HR person) who manages the platform — approving submissions, creating content, and adjusting settings. |

## 2. Getting Started — Signing Up & Logging In

### 2.1 Creating Your Account (Registration)

> **Important:** Registration may be controlled by your company administrator. If the "Create account" option is not visible on the login page, contact your HR or IT department to request access.

**Steps to register:**

1. **Open the GreenLoop website** in your web browser and press Enter. You will see the Login page.
2. **Click "Don't have an account? Create one"** — the link is below the login form.
3. **Fill in your details:**
   - Work email address
   - A secure password (at least 8 characters; use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols for best security)
   - First name and last name
   - Employee ID (ask HR if you are unsure of this number)
   - Department (select from the dropdown)
   - Job title
4. **Click "Create account"** to submit the form.
5. **Check your work email inbox** — a verification email from GreenLoop will arrive shortly.
6. **Click the verification link** inside the email. This confirms your email address and activates your account.
7. **You are now registered.** You will be taken to the Login page. Enter your email and password to sign in.

---

### 2.2 Logging In

1. **Go to the GreenLoop website** in your browser.
2. **Enter your email address and password** into the fields on the Login page.
3. **Click "Sign in."** You will be taken directly to your Dashboard.

> **Tip:** Your browser may offer to save your password. This is convenient but only do so on a personal, private device.

---

### 2.3 Forgotten Your Password?

1. On the login page, click the **"Forgot password?"** link.
2. Enter the **email address** associated with your GreenLoop account and submit.
3. **Check your inbox** — a secure password reset link will be emailed to you.
4. Click the link and **enter a new password** (and confirm it).
5. Click **Save.** You can now log in with your new password.

> **Note:** The reset link expires after a set amount of time (usually 1 hour). If it has expired, simply repeat the process to request a new link.

---

### 2.4 Accepting an Invitation

If an administrator has invited you to join the platform by email:

1. Open the **invitation email** you received.
2. Click the **invitation link** inside the email.
3. You will be taken to a page to **set your password** and confirm your details.
4. After completing the form, click **Accept Invitation.**
5. You can now log in normally using your email and the password you just set.

---

### 2.5 Logging Out

To sign out of GreenLoop:

- Click your **name or avatar** in the top navigation bar (or at the bottom of the sidebar) and select **"Sign out"** or **"Logout."**

> **Good practice:** Always log out when using a shared or public computer to protect your account.

## 3. Your Dashboard — The Home Screen

The Dashboard is the first page you see every time you log in. It is your personal sustainability command centre — a single page that gives you an at-a-glance overview of your progress and quick access to the most important parts of the platform.

### 3.1 What You See on the Dashboard

| Element | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Welcome message** | A personalised greeting using your first name, plus a motivational sustainability quote that changes based on your total points. |
| **Latest Announcements** | The two most recent company announcements published by administrators. These may include news, challenge launches, or sustainability milestones. |
| **Nature's Wisdom** | The two most recent educational articles or tips added by your company — topics like recycling guides or energy-saving tips. |
| **Stats row** | Four metric cards: Total Points, Green Score, Weekly Actions (logged in the past 7 days), and Badges Earned. |
| **Quick Actions** | A shortcut panel showing three available sustainability actions you can log straight from the Dashboard. Each shows the name, description, points value, and a "Log" button. |
| **Recent Badges** | Your three most recently earned achievement badges, with the date you earned them. |
| **Recent Activity** | A timeline of your five most recent logged actions and any recent challenge progress. |

---

### 3.2 Navigating with the Sidebar

On the left-hand side of every page, you will see a vertical navigation menu called the **sidebar.** It contains links to every section of the platform, grouped into three categories:

- **Main:** Dashboard, Actions, Challenges, Teams
- **Engagement:** Rewards, Badges, Leaderboard
- **Resources:** Announcements, Education, Analytics

At the bottom of the sidebar, you will also find links to your Profile, Settings, and the Sign Out option.

On smaller screens (phones and tablets), the sidebar may be hidden. Tap the **menu icon** (three horizontal lines) in the top left corner to open it.

> **Tip:** You can collapse the sidebar by clicking the collapse arrow at its edge. This gives you more reading space on your screen. The platform remembers your preference.

---

### 3.3 Understanding Your Level Progress

Your current level and total points are shown in the stats row. A progress bar on the Dashboard (and the Rewards page) shows exactly how close you are to reaching your next level. When you earn enough points, your level increases automatically. Reaching a new level unlocks new rewards you can claim.

## 4. Sustainability Actions — Earn Points by Going Green

Actions are the core of GreenLoop. The Actions page contains a library of eco-friendly activities your company has created. Every time you do something sustainable — cycle to work, reduce printing, choose a plant-based lunch — you can log it here and earn points toward your level.

### 4.1 How to Browse Actions

Click **"Actions"** in the left navigation menu. You will see a grid of action cards. Each card shows:

| Card element | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Action name** | A short title, e.g. "Cycle to work." |
| **Category badge** | Which sustainability area this belongs to (e.g. Transportation, Energy, Waste Reduction). |
| **Description** | A brief explanation of what the action involves. |
| **Points value** | How many points you earn for logging this action (shown in green, e.g. "+25 pts"). |
| **Green Score** | The estimated environmental impact (CO₂ equivalent saved) for this action. |
| **Difficulty** | A star rating from 1 (very easy) to 5 (challenging) showing how much effort the action typically requires. |
| **"Completed" badge** | If you have already logged this action in the past 7 days, a green "Completed" badge will appear as a reminder. |

---

### 4.2 Filtering and Searching for Actions

A search and filter bar appears at the top of the Actions page. You can:

- **Type** in the search box to find actions by name, description, or keyword.
- **Filter by category** — e.g. show only "Transportation" or "Energy" actions.
- **Filter by difficulty level** — choose 1 through 5 or any range.
- **Filter by points range** — e.g. show only high-value actions (100+ points).
- **Filter by Green Score range** — to focus on high-environmental-impact actions.
- **Switch tabs** at the top: **All Actions** (every action in the library), **Personal Actions** (actions you submitted yourself), or individual category tabs.

---

### 4.3 Logging an Action — Step by Step

1. **Find the action** you want to log by browsing or using the search/filter tools.
2. **Click the "Log Action" button** on the action card (it is the green button on each card).
3. **Fill in the submission form** that appears:
   - Confirm which action you are logging.
   - Add an optional note explaining what you did (e.g. "Cycled 8km to the office this morning").
   - Upload proof if required — this may be a photo, receipt, or document depending on what the action requires.
4. **Click "Submit."** Your submission will be sent for review.
5. **Wait for approval.** An administrator will review your submission. Once approved, the points are added to your account automatically and you will receive a notification.

> **Note:** Some actions may be set to "auto-approve" by your administrator, meaning points are awarded instantly without waiting for a manual review.

---

### 4.4 Submitting Your Own Personal Action

If you regularly do something sustainable that is not in the existing action library, you can propose it for review:

1. **Click "Submit My Action"** — find this button near the top of the Actions page.
2. **Describe your action:**
   - Enter a clear title (e.g. "Maintain a desk plant").
   - Write a description explaining what you do and why it is sustainable.
   - Choose the most relevant category from the dropdown.
   - Suggest a points value (the admin can adjust this).
3. **Click "Submit."** An administrator will review your proposal.
   - If **approved**, the action will appear on your **Personal** tab and you can log it like any other action.
   - If **not approved**, the admin may provide a reason for the decision.

---

### 4.5 Action Categories

Actions on GreenLoop are organised into these sustainability categories:

- Transportation
- Energy
- Water Conservation
- Waste Reduction
- Food & Diet
- Office Practices
- Community
- Digital

## 5. Challenges — Compete & Collaborate

Challenges are special, time-limited goals with a start date and an end date. Completing a challenge earns you **bonus points** on top of your regular action points. They are a great way to push yourself further and engage with colleagues.

### 5.1 Types of Challenges

**Personal Challenges**
Individual challenges created by you or by your administrator. Only you can see and participate in your own personal challenges. They are ideal for setting private goals, such as "Bring a reusable cup every day for 2 weeks."

**Team Challenges**
A challenge assigned to a specific team. Only members of that team can see and join the challenge. Your progress contributes to your team's overall score. These will only appear if you are a member of the relevant team.

**Company-Wide Challenges**
Open to everyone in the organisation. These are typically launched by administrators for major sustainability initiatives, awareness days, or company-wide events (e.g. "Go car-free for a week").

---

### 5.2 How to Browse Challenges

Click **"Challenges"** in the navigation menu. The page has three tabs:

- **Active Challenges** — all challenges currently open and available to you, divided into Personal, Team, and Company sections.
- **My Challenges** — only the challenges you have joined, showing your personal progress.
- **Completed** — challenges you have fully finished, with bonus points earned.

Each challenge card shows:
- Challenge name and description
- Start and end dates
- Type (Personal / Team / Company)
- Number of participants
- Reward points for completing it
- Your progress bar (if you have joined)

---

### 5.3 Joining a Challenge — Step by Step

1. Go to the **Challenges** page using the navigation menu.
2. Browse the **Active Challenges** tab.
3. Find a challenge you would like to participate in.
4. Click **"Join Challenge"** on the challenge card.
5. Your eligible logged actions will now **automatically count towards this challenge's progress.** You do not need to do anything extra — just keep logging your sustainability actions as normal.

> **Note:** You can join multiple challenges at the same time. Each eligible action you log will contribute to all relevant active challenges simultaneously.

---

### 5.4 Creating Your Own Challenge

> **Note:** Challenge creation may be enabled or disabled by your administrator. If the "Create Challenge" button is not visible, your company has currently restricted this feature for regular users.

1. Click **"Create Challenge"** — the button appears at the top right of the Challenges page.
2. Fill in the challenge details:
   - **Title:** A short, clear name for the challenge.
   - **Description:** Explain what participants need to do.
   - **Category:** Choose the sustainability category it relates to.
   - **Start Date** and **End Date:** Set when the challenge runs.
   - **Target Value:** The goal participants need to reach (e.g. complete 20 actions).
   - **Reward Points:** Bonus points awarded when the challenge is completed.
3. **Choose the type:** Personal or Team (administrators can also set Company-wide).
4. Click **"Create."** The challenge appears on your Challenges page immediately.

---

### 5.5 Tracking Your Challenge Progress

- The **My Challenges** tab shows all challenges you have joined, with a progress bar showing how close you are to completing each one.
- When your progress reaches 100%, the challenge is marked as **Completed,** bonus points are added to your account, and you receive a notification.
- The **Completed** tab shows all finished challenges and the bonus points you earned.

---

### 5.6 Leaving a Challenge

If you decide a challenge is not for you, you can leave it before it ends:

- Click the **"Leave Challenge"** button on the challenge card in the My Challenges tab.

> **Warning:** Leaving a challenge removes all your progress for that challenge. You will no longer count towards the challenge's completion, and any partial credit will be lost.

## 6. Teams — Work Together

Teams allow groups of colleagues to work together on sustainability challenges. Being part of a team is optional, but team challenges can earn extra points and it makes the experience more social and collaborative.

### 6.1 Viewing Teams

Click **"Teams"** in the navigation menu. The page has two sections:

**My Teams**
Shows all teams you currently belong to. For each team, you can see:
- Team name and description
- Member avatars and the total number of members
- Your role: **Member** or **Team Leader**
- Team stats: combined points and Green Score

**All Teams**
Shows every team registered on the platform. You can browse to find teams to join or see how other teams are performing.

---

### 6.2 Joining a Team — Step by Step

1. Go to the **All Teams** section on the Teams page.
2. Browse to find a team you would like to join.
3. Click on the team's card to open the **team detail page,** which shows the team's description, current members, and stats.
4. Click **"Join Team"** to become a member.

> **Note:** Teams have a maximum size set by the administrator. If a team is already full, the "Join Team" button will not be available for that team.

---

### 6.3 Creating a Team

> **Note:** Team creation may be disabled by your administrator. If you cannot see the "Create Team" button, contact your admin to enable this feature or create a team on your behalf.

1. Click **"Create Team"** at the top of the Teams page.
2. Enter a **team name** and a short **description** of your team's focus or sustainability goal.
3. Click **"Create."** You will automatically become the **Team Leader.**
4. Your team now appears in the All Teams list. Colleagues can find it and join, or you can ask an administrator to add specific people.

---

### 6.4 Team Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Participate in team challenges. Your logged actions contribute to team progress. |
| **Team Leader** | Created the team. Leaders can manage team membership (with administrator tools). They appear at the top of the team member list. |

---

### 6.5 Viewing a Team's Detail Page

Click on any team (from either My Teams or All Teams) to open the team detail page. Here you can see:
- Full team description
- Complete list of all members, with their individual point totals and Green Scores
- The team's combined total points and Green Score
- Any team challenges the team is participating in

## 7. Badges & Achievements — Your Milestone Rewards

Badges are digital awards that are **automatically granted** when you reach certain sustainability milestones. You do not need to claim them — the platform checks your progress continuously and awards each badge the moment you qualify.

### 7.1 Viewing Your Badges

Click **"Badges"** in the navigation menu to open the Achievement Garden.

At the top you will see three summary cards:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Badges Earned** | The number of badges you have earned, out of the total available on the platform. |
| **Growth Progress** | A percentage showing how far through the complete badge collection you have come. |
| **Next Milestone** | The name of the next badge you are closest to earning. |

Below the summary, every badge is displayed as a card:
- **Earned badges** appear in full colour with a green tick icon and the date you earned them.
- **Locked badges** appear greyed out with a padlock icon, a description of what you need to do, and a progress bar showing how close you are.

---

### 7.2 How Badges Are Earned

There are three types of badge criteria:

| Criteria type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Points-based** | "Earn 500 total points." |
| **Actions-based** | "Complete 10 approved sustainability actions." |
| **Green Score-based** | "Save 50 units of CO₂ equivalent." |

Each badge card shows its exact criteria in an orange label (e.g. "Earn 100 points") so you always know what is needed.

> **Tip:** Check the progress bars on locked badges regularly. When a bar is nearly full, you are close to earning that badge — a few more actions could tip you over the line!

## 8. Rewards — Claim Your Real-World Prizes

The Rewards page is where earning points becomes tangible. As you earn points and progress through levels, you unlock real rewards that your company has prepared. These can be physical gifts, digital vouchers, experiences, or workplace privileges.

### 8.1 Types of Rewards

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | A physical item sent to you — for example an eco-friendly product, reusable water bottle, or gift hamper. |
| **Digital** | A digital reward such as a voucher code, e-gift card, or online subscription. |
| **Experience** | An activity or event — such as a team lunch, wellness session, or sustainability workshop. |
| **Privilege** | A workplace benefit — for example an extra day off, flexible working arrangement, or public recognition. |

---

### 8.2 Claiming a Reward — Step by Step

1. Click **"Rewards"** in the navigation menu. This opens the Level Rewards page.
2. At the top of the page, a **progress card** shows your total points, current level, and how many points you need to reach the next level.
3. Click the **"Available Rewards"** tab to see all rewards you are currently eligible to claim based on your level.
4. Read the details on each reward card: the reward name, the level required, the reward type, and a description.
5. Click **"Claim Reward"** on the reward you want.
6. Your claim is submitted. An administrator will review and process it. **You will receive a follow-up email or notification within 24–48 business hours** explaining the next steps (e.g. where to collect a physical item, or how to receive a digital code).

> **Important:** Each level reward can only be claimed once. Once claimed, the reward moves to your "Claimed Rewards" tab and cannot be claimed again.

---

### 8.3 Tracking Your Claimed Rewards

Open the **"Reward Status"** tab on the Rewards page to see the status of all your reward claims:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your claim has been submitted and is waiting for an administrator to review it. |
| **Approved** | An admin has approved your claim. Your reward is being prepared or sourced. |
| **Delivered** | Your reward has been sent to you or made available for collection. |
| **Rejected** | Your claim was not approved. Any notes from the admin explaining the reason will be shown here. |

## 9. Leaderboard — See How You Rank

The Leaderboard shows how you compare with colleagues across multiple sustainability categories. It is designed to inspire healthy, friendly competition and to celebrate the most active and impactful contributors.

Your own row is always **highlighted** so you can quickly spot your current position at a glance.

### 9.1 Your Ranking Summary

At the top of the Leaderboard page, a card shows your current position in three categories:

| Category | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Points Ranking** | Your position among all users based on total accumulated points. |
| **Green Score Ranking** | Your position based on total environmental impact (CO₂ saved). |
| **Monthly Actions** | Your position based on how many approved actions you have completed in the past 30 days. |

> **Note:** Rankings show the top 10. If you are not yet in the top 10 for a category, "N/A" will be shown — keep logging actions to climb the leaderboard!

---

### 9.2 Leaderboard Tabs

**Points**
Top 10 users ranked by all-time total points. Each row shows the user's name, department, current level, and total points. Gold, silver, and bronze icons appear for positions 1, 2, and 3.

**Green Score**
Top 10 users ranked by cumulative Green Score (environmental impact). This is the best reflection of real-world sustainability impact.

**Monthly Actions**
Top 10 most active users in the past 30 days, ranked by number of completed and approved actions. This **resets each month**, giving everyone a fresh opportunity to lead.

**Departments**
Rankings grouped by department, rather than by individual. Shows which part of your company is collectively the most sustainable. Your own department row is highlighted.

### 10.1 Types of Notifications

### 10.2 Viewing Your Notifications

Each notification shows:
- An icon indicating the notification type
- A bold title and a message explaining what happened
- A timestamp showing how long ago it was sent (e.g. "2 hours ago")
- A link to the relevant page if applicable (e.g. click to go straight to the challenge you completed)
- **Unread notifications** appear with a bold title and a coloured dot on the left

---

### 10.3 Marking Notifications as Read

- **Click on any notification** to mark it as read and open the related content.
- Use the **"Mark all as read"** button at the top of the Notifications page to clear all unread notifications at once.
- Use the **"Mark all as unread"** option if you want to revisit them later.

## 12. Account Settings — Notifications, Privacy & Data

The Settings page lets you control what notifications you receive, manage your privacy preferences, and handle your account data.

### 12.1 Accessing Settings

Click **"Settings"** in the left navigation sidebar.

---

### 12.2 Notification Preferences

The **Notifications** tab shows a list of notification types, each with a toggle switch. Simply click a toggle to turn a notification type on or off.

| Notification Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Action Status | On | Notify me when my action submissions are approved or rejected. |
| Challenge Progress | On | Notify me about milestones and progress in my challenges. |
| Team Updates | On | Notify me about changes to my teams. |
| Announcements | On | Notify me about new company announcements. |
| Educational Content | On | Notify me when new learning articles are posted. |
| Reward Status | On | Notify me when my reward claims are updated. |
| Achievement Alerts | On | Notify me when I earn a new badge. |
| Leaderboard Updates | Off | Notify me when my leaderboard ranking changes. |

---

### 12.3 Privacy Settings

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Profile Visibility** | Public | Choose whether your profile is visible to all platform users or kept private. |
| **Leaderboard Participation** | On | Choose whether your name appears on the public leaderboard. If turned off, you will not appear in rankings. |
| **Analytics Sharing** | On | Allow the platform to include your data in anonymised company-wide analytics reports. |

---

### 12.4 Exporting Your Data

You can download a copy of all your personal data stored on GreenLoop:

1. Go to the **Data** tab within the Settings page.
2. Click **"Export My Data."**
3. A JSON file will be downloaded to your computer. This file contains all your personal information, logged actions, and points history in a standard, readable format.

> **What is a JSON file?** It is a text file that stores data in a structured format. You can open it with any text editor (like Notepad on Windows or TextEdit on Mac) to read through your records. It can also be opened and interpreted by spreadsheet software.

### 12.5 Deleting Your Account

> **Warning: This is permanent and cannot be undone.** All your points, badges, action history, and personal data will be permanently removed from the platform. Only proceed if you are absolutely certain.

To delete your account:
1. Go to the **Settings** page.
2. Find the **"Delete Account"** section.
3. Type the word `DELETE` into the confirmation box (this is to make sure you do not do this by accident).
4. Click **"Delete My Account."**
5. You will be signed out immediately and your account will be permanently removed.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: I logged an action but my points have not appeared yet. What should I do?**

A: If your action was submitted (and not auto-approved), it is waiting for an administrator to review it. This can take up to a few business days depending on your company's review schedule. You will receive a notification as soon as it is approved and your points are awarded. If you believe there is a problem after several days, contact your system administrator.

---

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**

A: Go to the GreenLoop login page and click "Forgot password?" Enter your registered work email address and you will receive a password reset link. Click the link in the email and set a new password. The link is time-limited — if it has expired, repeat the process.

---

**Q: Can I log the same action multiple times?**

A: Yes. You can log the same action multiple times, subject to any frequency limits set by your administrator (e.g. some actions can only be logged once per week). If an action was already completed in the past 7 days, a "Completed" badge appears on it as a reminder — you can still log it again if the limit permits.

---

**Q: Why can I not see a "Create Team" button?**

A: Team creation can be enabled or disabled by your administrator. If the button is not visible, this feature is currently turned off for regular users. Contact your administrator if you would like a team created.

---

**Q: My action was rejected. What happens now?**

A: You will receive a notification explaining that the submission was rejected, along with any reason the administrator provided. No points will be awarded. You are welcome to re-submit the action with additional information or proof if you believe it was rejected in error.

---

**Q: How do I earn a badge?**

A: Badges are awarded automatically when you reach a specific milestone. Each badge shows its exact criteria on its card (e.g. "Earn 500 points" or "Complete 10 approved actions"). Go to the Badges page at any time to see all badges and the progress bar for each one. You do not need to take any special action — the platform awards the badge the moment you qualify.

---

**Q: I claimed a reward but have not heard anything. What should I do?**

A: After claiming a reward, you should receive a follow-up notification or email from an administrator within 24–48 business hours. If you have not heard back after 48 hours, go to Rewards → Reward Status and check if your claim is still marked as "Pending." If so, contact your administrator directly.

---

**Q: Can I join more than one team?**

A: Yes. There is no restriction on how many teams you can join. However, each team may have a maximum member limit set by the administrator, so some teams may be full.

---

**Q: Will other people be able to see my name on the leaderboard?**

A: By default, yes — your name and ranking appear on the public leaderboard. If you would prefer not to appear, go to Settings → Privacy and turn off "Leaderboard Participation." Your actions will still be counted in anonymised company-wide analytics, but your name will not be shown publicly in rankings.

---

**Q: What is my Green Score? Is it different from my points?**

A: Yes, they are different measures.
- **Points** are a gamification score — they determine your level and leaderboard ranking.
- **Green Score** represents the estimated real-world environmental impact of your actions, measured in CO₂ equivalent units saved.

Both go up as you log actions, but they are tracked separately. An action might give you 50 points but have a Green Score of 5 (a low-impact action), or 20 points but a Green Score of 30 (a high-impact action).

---

**Q: I do not see a Register option on the login page. How do I sign up?**

A: Your company administrator has disabled self-registration. This means new users can only be added by invitation. Contact your HR department or system administrator and ask them to invite you to the platform.

---

**Q: How do I change my email address?**

A: Email addresses cannot be changed directly within the platform. Please contact your system administrator, who can update your email in the Admin Users panel.

---

**Q: I accidentally deleted my account. Can it be restored?**

A: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone through the platform. All points, badges, and action history are permanently removed. If you deleted your account by mistake, contact your system administrator as soon as possible — they may be able to assist, though restoration is not guaranteed.

---

**Q: Is my personal data safe?**

A: GreenLoop takes data privacy seriously. All data is stored securely and access is controlled using Row Level Security (RLS) policies — meaning each user can only see their own data. Administrators can access platform-wide data for management purposes only. You can export or permanently delete your own data at any time from the Settings page.

---

**Q: Can I use GreenLoop on my phone?**

A: Yes. GreenLoop is a web-based platform that is fully responsive, meaning it adapts to work on phones, tablets, and computers. Simply open your phone's web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.), navigate to the GreenLoop address, and log in as normal. No app download is required.
